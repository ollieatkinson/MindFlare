# MindFlare

MindFlare is a macOS app for exploring, shaping, and connecting Lexicon knowledge graphs.

It turns names, ideas, terms, schemas, notes, and relationships into a fast document-based workspace: a place to browse large semantic structures, follow inheritance, compose related lexicons, and search through the meaning around a term instead of only the text of its name.

## A Home For Names That Matter

Naming is where systems become real. MindFlare is built for the moment when a list of names becomes a shared language: product concepts, domain models, API schemas, feature taxonomies, glossaries, ontologies, generated namespaces, or a personal map of thought.

Instead of flattening those names into a spreadsheet or hiding them in code, MindFlare lets you move through them as a living graph.

## What You Can Do

- Browse deep lexicons with a column interface that stays fast even for large documents.
- Model inheritance so one term can carry meaning from another without duplicating structure.
- Compose lexicons from local files and related documents.
- Search by path, name, context, notes, comments, and semantic relevance.
- Inspect connected and inherited nodes without losing your place.
- Keep your work in regular document files that can live with the rest of your project or knowledge base.

## Why It Exists

MindFlare is for people and teams who need their shared language to be visible, navigable, and useful:

- product teams shaping feature and capability maps
- engineers designing domain schemas and API vocabularies
- writers and researchers organizing large conceptual spaces
- teams building ontologies, glossaries, and taxonomies
- anyone turning repeated naming decisions into a durable system

## Built On Lexicon

MindFlare edits [Lexicon](https://github.com/ollieatkinson/Lexicon) documents: composable knowledge graphs designed for semantic structure, inheritance, connections, and generation.

The app is the visual workspace; Lexicon is the underlying format and engine.
